Clan Power

Overview

"Clan power determines what Clan Bonuses you can have active that affects all clan members." Leaders, Quartermasters, and certain regular clan members can modify clan power at Royal University (coordinates 2,2).

Clan standings are viewable through the Leaderboards in the Clan section.

Gaining Clan Power

Players typically begin with 0-1 power. As individual strength increases, so does that player's value to their clan, unlocking access to stronger bonuses. "For Ironman accounts, clan power is multiplied by 5x."
