Wedding

Overview

In order to marry someone, you must both have a wedding ring equipped. To propose to someone, you use the in-game command /wed [player name]. That player will receive your proposal and can respond with /yesido. Doing so will display your partner's ID through /id [player], announce it to all players, and display in the married players section of the Leaderboards.

This is completely cosmetic and has no bonuses.
