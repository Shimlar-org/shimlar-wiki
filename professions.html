Professions

Professions serve as a secondary specialization mechanism, providing mastery bonuses, stat increases, and unique perks. They remain accessible until your character level reaches 5 times the profession's requirement level.

Profession Management

Characters can modify their profession at Royal University (coordinates 1,2) for a fee of 1 million gold below level 1,000, or 10 million gold thereafter. This change does not reset character progression. However, downgrading professions may result in lost mastery bonuses if you've reached maximum mastery levels.

Certain professions grant Class Type Bonuses—applicable to all classes except Sharpshooter, Vampire, and Werewolf. These include Mage Spirit, Warrior Spirit, Black Mage, Mercenary, White Mage, Assassin, Supreme Sorcerer, and Saboteur.

Access the profession menu using "/professions" or "/pf" commands.
