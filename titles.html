Titles

Overview

Titles serve as a customization feature earned through gameplay objectives and appear before your name in chat windows. The system includes five tiers per title—Silver, Limegreen, Cyan, Crimson, and Gold—with increasing difficulty. Most titles are universally available, though some have restrictions or single-player locks. "When a title is used, your highest rank color is applied."

Setting a Title

Access /titles to view unlocked titles marked "completed." Note the ID from the first column to set your desired title using /settitle [ID]. Examples include /settitle 2 for the Slayer title and /settitle 0 to remove your current title. Notably, "/settitle wed will set your title to your partner. Some people have gotten creative with marrying a dummy account to basically create a custom title."
