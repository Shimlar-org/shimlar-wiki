Zone Finder

Enter your level to see the zones you can enter, then sort them by the best experience, gold or monster stats on offer. Leave the level blank to list every zone.

ZoneLevelTypeTop experienceTop goldTop stats

How to read this

  • Zones are listed if their level is at or below the level you enter. A zone's level is the minimum level needed to enter it.
  • "Top" columns show the best single monster in that zone: the highest experience, gold and stats value among its monsters.
  • This tool does not know your own stats, gear or how hard a fight will be. Use it to compare rewards, then check the zone's full list on Monster Stats.
Figures compiled from the game's zone pages at shimlar.org/maps and the guide's gem drop rules. Values can change when the game is updated. Unofficial fan reference, not affiliated with Shimlar Ltd.
