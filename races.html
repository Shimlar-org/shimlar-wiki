Races

There are 13 different races to choose from, with each having 3 evolutions. Each race specializes in a particular weapon or spell class, although you're not limited to just that one type.

11 races are playable at the start of the game, with the final two available in the later stages of the game. Each race starts out as Tier 1, and you can Reincarnate to advance to the next tier, assuming you meet the proper requirements.

Reincarnation System

You start out as the first tier of whichever race you choose when creating your character. To upgrade your character to grow stronger, you must reincarnate! To do this, you go to 2,1 in Royal University. If you meet the requirements, you can choose to evolve into a stronger race. You lose 5% of your levels when you reincarnate, so be mindful of gear requirements! Evolving into a stronger race enables you to gain more stats per level and raise your mastery cap.
