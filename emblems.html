Emblems

Overview

Emblems function as exchangeable gem slots that provide positive effects alongside negative effects (typically of equivalent grade), requiring only gold expenditure and allowing switches at any time with sufficient funds. The command "/emblems" activates this feature.

Purchasing Emblems

To acquire emblems, visit coordinates 0,4 in Bladeforge to select and purchase. "There are no refunds when switching emblems." Each emblem displays its own color that modifies your name appearance in chat (adjustable via Game Settings).

Purchasing alone doesn't activate effects; each emblem includes one or more effects to apply.

Enchantments

Access enchantments at 4,3 in Enchanted Forest to select tier levels. "The tier you choose is the same tier of gem that will apply." For instance, selecting an enchantment with +Dragon Fang and -Frozen Tear at Tier 3 grants weapon class +3 and armor class -3 based on gem values.

Emblems follow Gem Restrictions. Important note: "Vampires do not gain any armor bonuses from emblems, but they do gain Item Class from demon fang enchantments."
