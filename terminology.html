Terminology

A glossary of terms and acronyms used in Shimlar.

Weapons & Spells

  • Weapons: "Classified as Sword, Axe, Staff, Mace, Bow, Arrow, Shield, Claw"
  • Spells: "Classified as Fire, Cold, Air/Lightning, Earth, Drain, Arcane"

Damage & Accuracy Buffs

  • Item Class (IC): "Applies a damage buff to any weapon or spell"
  • Weapon Class (WC): "Applies a damage buff to only weapons"
  • Spell Class (SC): "Applies a damage buff to only spells"
  • Hit Chance (HC): "Applies an accuracy buff to any weapon or spell"
  • Weapon Hit Chance (WHC): "Applies an accuracy buff to only weapons"
  • Spell Hit Chance (SHC): "Applies an accuracy buff to only spells"

Defense

  • Armor Class (AC): "Applies a damage reduction buff to armor"

Core Attributes

  • STR: "Strength, one of the core attributes of the game that increases the damage of weapons"
  • DEX: "Dexterity, one of the core attributes of the game that increases the accuracy of weapons"
  • NTL: "Intelligence, one of the core attributes of the game that increases the damage of spells"
  • WIS: "Wisdom, of the core attributes of the game that increases the accuracy of spells"
  • VIT: "Vitality, one of the core attributes of the game that increases your health pool"
  • HEALTH: "One of the core attributes of the game that is your current Health before death"

Combat Effects

  • Critical Hit Chance: "On hit, percentage chance to perform more damage than usual"
  • Critical Hit Damage: "On hit, increases your damage of a critical hit"
  • Enemy Hit Chance: "On hit, decreases the chance an enemy lands a blow"
  • Double Hit (DH): "Hit the enemy more often, possibly triggering a double hit mastery"
  • Damage Multiplier (DM): "Exclusive to werewolfs, basically acts as a damage bonus similar to crits"

Equipment Slots

  • Right Hand (RH): "Slot for a primary weapon"
  • Left Hand (LH): "Slot for a secondary weapon"
  • Spell One (SO): "Slot for a primary spell"
  • Spell Two (ST): "Slot for a secondary spell"

Drops & Multipliers

  • Resource Drop Chance: "On kill, increases chance to drop one of the 9 resources"
  • Bonus Time (BT): "Game multipliers that speed up Exp, Gold, Masteries, Gem Drops, Shadow Drops" and other rewards
  • Personal Bonus Time (PBT): "Game multipliers that speed up Exp, Gold, Masteries, Gem Drops, Shadow Drops" for individual characters
  • Drop Chance (DC): "Increases your chances of dropping a gem, shadow, purse, clan mine, and resource drops"
  • Random Drop Chance (RDC): "Increases your chances of dropping one of either a gem, shadow, purse, clan mine, or resource drop"
