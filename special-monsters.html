Special Monsters

Introduction

While killing monsters in any zone, you may come across monsters that have special titles next to their name. Kill them for an increased reward or potential for a random chance at a drop! Be careful though, a lot of them are tougher than their regular counterparts, and some will outright kill you with no way to defend yourself!

Finding Specials

The base chance to discover a special monster upon every new fight is 1/250. These odds can be improved through Accessories or Clan Tracker Academy. Anything designated with +Special Monsters indicates this buff, which includes Circlet of the Savant, Circlet of Knowledge, Circlet of the King, and Circlet of the Gods.

Special List

There are currently 33 special monsters in the game, which can be found in any zone with a few exceptions:

  • Special Monsters are disabled until you are Level 25+
  • Due to Conquests, the Open Water and Shadow Forest zones do not contain the following specials: Wraith, Champion, Glutton, Warlock, Executioner, Overlord, Guardian, Scoundrel, Maniac, Spectre, Colossus, Harlot, or Reaper.

There are 5 tiers associated with special monsters, with each Tier weighted so you find them less often. Your clan tracker academy can help normalize higher tier special monsters to become more common, along with certain professions as well.

Mob Enhancements (Tier 1)

  • Double Hit means they have a chance to hit you more times than normal
