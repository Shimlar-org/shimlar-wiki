Gem Drop Calculator

These tools turn the rules in Gem Drop Algorithm into numbers. Every figure is a plain chance per gem drop, worked out from the percentages on that page.

How many drops will it take?

Pick a pool, then see how likely it is to appear in a number of drops, and how many drops it usually takes.

How the odds work

  • A random number from 1 to 100 picks the pool: 1 to 40 is 40%, 41 to 65 is 25%, 66 to 85 is 20%, 86 to 95 is 10%, 96 to 99 is 4%, and 100 is 1%.
  • Only the roll of 100 reaches Dragon Tear, Demon Tear and Pebble. A second roll from 1 to 10 then decides: 1 to 3 (30%) gives a Tear, and 4 to 10 (70%) gives a Pebble.
  • The guide says a Tear has a 12.5% chance of being a Moonfang instead. This calculator applies that 12.5% to the 30% Tear roll, and treats Dragon Tear and Demon Tear together because the guide does not say how they are split.
  • For the pools of 40%, 25%, 20%, 10% and 4%, the calculator gives the chance of that whole pool, not one named gem, because the guide does not say how gems inside a pool are weighted. Vampire bonuses and class gem conversion are not included.

Gem grade chances

Every gem starts at the lowest grade the zone drops. For each extra grade the zone allows, there is a 33% chance to move up one grade. Enter the lowest and highest grade the zone drops.

GradeChance

Dragon Tear and Demon Tear always come in the highest grade the zone allows.
