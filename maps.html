Maps

An interactive map of every zone in Shimlar. The left side shows how all 168 zones connect through portals. Click a zone, or search for one, and its own grid opens on the right with every bank, shop, resource spot and portal in place.

Find a route between two zones

How to read the map

  • World view. Each dot is a zone, coloured by its zone type, and each line is a portal link. The Wilderness sits at the centre because most of the outer map is reached from it. Positions on screen are only a layout to keep the dots apart. Shimlar has no fixed world geography, so distance on the diagram does not mean distance in the game.
  • Zone grid. Squares are numbered as in the game: 0,0 is the bottom left, x grows to the right and y grows upwards, so north is up. Letters mark the kind of place. Click a square to see everything on it, or click a name in the key to hide that kind of place.
  • Filters. Zone type fades other types. Your level fades zones above it, since a zone's level is the minimum level to enter. Zones with fades zones that lack a bank, anvil, questboard and so on.
  • Routes. The route finder follows the portals listed on each zone's page and returns the route with the fewest portals. It does not check level requirements.

About the data

Everything on this page was compiled from the zone pages at shimlar.org/maps: grid sizes, portal and interaction coordinates, and zone levels and types. A spot check of 16 zones against the live pages found every portal and interaction row matching. Zone data can change when the game is updated, so confirm anything important in game.

  • A few zone pages list a place at a coordinate outside the zone's own grid. The map shows those below the grid as "listed without a square" instead of guessing where they belong.
  • The Sky Terminal has portals of its own, but no other zone's page lists a portal to it, so a route to it may include a link that is only listed from the far side.
  • Two tunnel zones near the Underground Network have one square each.
  • Zone pages can list the same place twice. Those entries are shown as listed.
