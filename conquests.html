Conquests

Conquests are basically side activities you can accomplish that give various perks you cannot gain elsewhere.

Drop Types

  • ZONE: Items drop only while in the conquest's designated zone.
  • ANYWHERE: Items drop regardless of current zone location.

General Features

  • All conquest rewards are permanent except for Gem Hunter.
  • There are 39 total conquests across various level ranges, with some designated as LEGENDARY for their length or cost relative to the rewards provided.
  • Conquest items do not share a drop pool with anything else - meaning you can drop a normal item and/or a resource drop as well as a conquest item.
  • Conquests that provide Masteries offer permanent increases to mastery caps.
