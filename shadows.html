Shadows

Shadows Overview

"Shadows are one of the cornerstone features of the game and a primary drop source to strengthen your character."

When defeating monsters, players have a chance to receive shadow drops, though this depends on zone parameters. Shadow drops compete in a shared pool with gems, purses, and clan mines—only one per kill.

Obtaining Shadows

To drop any shadow item, players must equip gear. The primary method involves:

Shop Gear Purchase: Visit The Guilded Anvil to buy and equip gear pieces, then begin defeating monsters to trigger shadow drops.

"Keep in mind though, if any of your gear slots do not contain a gear piece, you have a high chance of only dropping Item Class 1 gear."

How Shadows Work

Shadow drops are determined by equipped gear. Each monster kill triggers a potential drop message, with the system randomly selecting equipment to drop based on Item Class, differing by gear type.

Buy Gear + Each Tier

Five gear tiers exist in the game:

  • Shop Tier 1: Required for best shadows. The Guilded Anvil sells Weapons/Armor; The Arcanium sells Spells and enchantments.
  • Shadow Tier 2: Generally weaker than shop gear alone, but feature two gem slots and lower requirements. All tiers drop shadows.
  • Mythic Tier 3: Upgrade shadow gear at Temple of Shadows (2,1) for gold.
  • Ancestral Tier 4: Upgrade mythic gear using gold and fusion pieces at Fallen Temple (1,0) for enhanced power.
  • Godly Tier 5 (Final): Upgrade ancestral gear to the most powerful form at Temple of Ultimate Wisdom (8,7).

Shop Gear (T1)

Shop gear represents the game's foundation and most critical gear type. Purchase locations include The Guilded Anvil (Armor/Weapons) and The Arcanium (Spells, Enchantments).

Gear progresses from Item Class 1 through 25 (shown as Roman Numerals in-game), increasing in cost, stats, and level requirements.

  • IC1 and IC2 gear contain 1 gem slot
  • All other gear contains 2 gem slots

"What shop sells what is determined by the zone you're in. Here are the three zones that contain all gear."

Shop gear drives the best shadow drops. "The way shop gear works is you always drop either the same Item Class or one lower."

Example: Dropping from an Armor IV shadow yields either III or IV.

Shadow Gear (T2)

Shadow gear represents the initial upgrade path for maximum gear power. While shop gear generates the finest shadows, shadows themselves also drop shadows.

The key distinction: "You cannot drop the same Item Class as the equipped shadow."

Example: A Shadow Fire IV can only produce shadow I-III, never IV. Extra shadows may be sold to other players or smashed for fusion pieces at the Fusion Machine.

Shadow gear generally underperforms shop gear statistically but provides significantly lower equip requirements plus 2 gem slots across all types (compared to Shop Tier 1-2's single slot).

Mythic Gear (T3)

Two methods grant mythic gear: upgrading shadow gear or discovering them through Quests (rarely). Shadow-to-mythic upgrading occurs at Temple of Shadows (2,1).

"Simply select what shadow you want to upgrade, and pay the gold cost to upgrade. If you have more than 1 shadow in your inventory, you can also choose the mass-upgrade option to do it all at once."

Mythic IV drops follow the shadow rule—only I-III versions drop. "Mythic gear is the best bang for your buck of obtained fusion pieces at the Fusion Machine."

Ancestral Gear (T4)

Ancestral gear comes from upgrading mythic gear or rare Quest drops. Mythic-to-ancestral upgrading happens at Fallen Temple (1,0).

"While ancestral gear also applies the I-(MAX IC-1) rule for dropping compared to shop gear, you have a chance for the shadow to be instant Mythic level instead!"

Upgrades require both gold and fusion pieces, with gear needing to be VIII or higher. "A pop-up will appear confirming your upgrade - make sure you all meet requirements before proceeding, as ancestral gear contain higher requirements and stats to equip."

"While you can certainly smash ancestral gear for fusion pieces at the Fusion Machine, it usually isn't worth it since it cost FP to upgrade."

Godly Gear (T5)

Three paths lead to godly gear: upgrading ancestral gear, duping existing godly gear, or rare Quest finds. Ancestral-to-godly upgrading occurs at Temple of Ultimate Wisdom (8,7).

"While godly gear still follows the rule of dropping I-(MAX IC-1) rule, there are two additional perks (on top of a big power boost): drops are always mythic level, and you have a small chance to dupe your godly item. You can fail a dupe though, in which you get an Item Class 1 version of your godly item instead."

Upgrades require gold and fusion pieces, with gear needing to be XII or higher. "A pop-up will appear confirming your upgrade - make sure you all meet requirements before proceeding, as godly gear contain much higher requirements and stats to equip."

"While you can certainly smash godly gear for fusion pieces at the Fusion Machine, it usually isn't worth it since it cost FP to upgrade."
