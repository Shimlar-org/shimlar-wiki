Roleplay Rules

Shimlar permits roleplay exclusively in the dedicated RP Chat channel. The RP Arch dispenses rewards (gold, levels, bonus time, etc.) at their discretion, only when actively monitoring roleplay. Unobserved RP yields no rewards. Players should not submit screenshots or documentation to claim awards.

Roleplay Rules

1. Observation and Participation

"Observe the Roleplay room for at least 5 minutes." Initiating RP is acceptable if the room is inactive. Joining existing roleplay requires prior DM permission. Characters may possess any abilities, though "God RPing" (portraying unaffected, all-powerful beings) results in muting.

2. Etiquette

  • Precede every roleplay line with "/e"
  • Exchange turns sequentially with partners
  • Use DMs for coordination
  • Romance content is permissible if non-explicit and plot-aligned; excessive romance-focused RPs result in muting and potential bans
  • Use "-c" notation for continued lines exceeding character limits
  • Develop multifaceted characters with vulnerabilities and fears, revealing traits progressively

3. Rewards

Eligible normal accounts may receive gold, experience, bonus time, or title boosts based on roleplay quality. Ironman accounts cannot earn rewards. Bonus Time runs continuously and expires if players don't utilize it before logging off.
