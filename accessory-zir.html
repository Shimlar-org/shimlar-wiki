Accessory Enchanting (Zir)

Overview

Upon receiving a shadow drop, there is a chance to obtain the message "The Gods created a new slot in your item!" This action adds a Zir gem to your currently equipped accessory, with potential for up to 9 levels (Zir1 through Zir9).

Players may visit location 0,2 in the Temple of Shadows to exchange a Zir gem for a gem of matching grade in return for Marbles. For instance, a Zir3 can be converted to any Grade 3 Gem variant.

Important note: Once a gem is slotted into an accessory, it cannot be changed. Players should verify their selection before confirming.
